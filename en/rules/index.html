Rules

This page describes the default rules. Number of games, scoring (simple or with bonus), card faces (German or French), starting dealer and time per turn can be changed in the app. Not included are Ramsch, Ouvert, Contra and Re, Bock rounds, and announcing Schneider or Schwarz.

Goal

Skat is played by three with 32 cards and 120 points in total. In every game one player, the declarer, plays against the other two. The declarer wins with at least 61 points (in null: without taking a single trick). A match consists of 12 games; afterwards the player with the most points wins. Scoring is in points, not money.

Cards and card points

Ace 11, Ten 10, King 4, Over 3, Under 2; Nine, Eight and Seven count nothing. The two skat cards count for the declarer at the end. French card faces: Ace 11, Ten 10, King 4, Queen 3, Jack 2; Nine, Eight and Seven count nothing. The two skat cards count for the declarer at the end.

Trumps

In suit games and grand the four Unders are trumps; the highest is the Under of acorns, then leaves, hearts, bells. In a suit game the cards of the chosen suit follow (Ace, Ten, King, Over, Nine, Eight, Seven). In the other suits the order is Ace, Ten, King, Over, Nine, Eight, Seven. In null there are no trumps; in every suit the order is Ace, King, Over, Under, Ten, Nine, Eight, Seven. French card faces: In suit games and grand the four Jacks are trumps; the highest is the Jack of clubs, then spades, hearts, diamonds. In a suit game the cards of the chosen suit follow (Ace, Ten, King, Queen, Nine, Eight, Seven). In the other suits the order is Ace, Ten, King, Queen, Nine, Eight, Seven. In null there are no trumps; in every suit the order is Ace, King, Queen, Jack, Ten, Nine, Eight, Seven.

Bidding

To the left of the dealer sit forehand and middlehand; the dealer is rearhand. Middlehand bids first, forehand holds with “yes” or passes. The winner then bids against rearhand. Values are multiples of the base values (18, 20, 22, 23, 24, 27 …). The last to hold plays. If everyone passed, forehand may still say 18 and play; otherwise the hand is passed in and dealt again (it counts as played, without points).

Skat and hand

The declarer may pick up the skat (the two face-down cards) and must then discard two cards; they count for the declarer. If the skat is left alone, the declarer plays hand, which raises the game value. Then the declarer announces the game.

Games

Suit game: acorns, leaves, hearts or bells and the Unders are trumps (base value 12, 11, 10, 9). Grand: only the Unders are trumps (base value 24). Null: no trumps; the declarer wins only by taking no trick (value 23, as hand 35). French card faces: Suit game: clubs, spades, hearts or diamonds and the Jacks are trumps (base value 12, 11, 10, 9). Grand: only the Jacks are trumps (base value 24). Null: no trumps; the declarer wins only by taking no trick (value 23, as hand 35).

Play

Forehand leads. You must follow suit, or trump if trump was led (in suit games and grand every Under belongs to trumps); if you cannot, play any card. The highest trump wins the trick or, without a trump, the highest card of the suit led. The winner leads the next trick.

Game value

The game value is the base value times a factor. The factor counts “with” or “without” that many Unders in a row (starting from the Under of acorns, in a suit game continuing through the trumps), plus one for the game, one for hand, one for schneider (the loser has at most 30 points) and one more for schwarz (the loser takes no trick). Example: hearts with 2, game 3, hand 4: 4 × 10 = 40. Whoever bids higher than the game is worth has overbid and loses. The declarer's ten dealt cards count and, if the skat was picked up, the two skat cards.

Scoring

Simple scoring (default): If the declarer wins, the game value is credited; if the declarer loses, double the game value is deducted. The opponents get nothing. Scoring is in points, not money. Scoring with bonus: If the declarer wins, the declarer gets 50 points plus the game value; if the declarer loses, 50 points plus double the game value are deducted and each opponent gets 40 points. Scoring is in points, not money.

Time per turn

You have 60 seconds per turn. When time runs out, a pass, picking up the skat, discarding, declaring a game or a legal card is played for you.

In the app

Tap the buttons when bidding, two hand cards when discarding and one card when playing. Only legal cards are highlighted. You see the points of each game after the last trick.

Several humans

If several humans share the device, hands are hidden after every move. Pass the device on; the next player taps the screen.

Computer

Computer players only know their own cards and what lies open on the table.

Skat is a game by Robert Peringer.
